## Scoring and continuation design

SQLite-native `DENSE_RANK` probes retain its NULL/collation semantics. Tied reference keys are marked
unordered, preserving duplicate multiplicity. Unresolved ordering without LIMIT is also unordered;
ambiguous/unresolved LIMIT/offset selections are excluded (30 tied boundaries, 14 unordered LIMITs,
one unsupported ordered LIMIT). Every policy is recorded. This detects top-level ordering ambiguity,
not every semantic ambiguity in nested SQL and not general query equivalence.

Ordinary 'now' strings, comments and quoted identifiers are allowed. Actual current-time functions
and clock-dependent date arguments fail closed, including arguments computed from expressions.
Read-only execution and the existing resource caps remain enforced.

The **10% gate is unique targets per pass on both sides**. Deduplicate trajectories/context for A and
verified B; exclude validation, repeated copies, epochs, optimizer updates and padding. Prepared-stage
denominators are frozen separately from actual cumulative training-cost tokens. Actual A usage and B
verified yield do not exist yet; the pre-sampling forecast still awaits an approved A dev result.

`match_control` matches globally over exactly B's retained questions using whole examples and at most
one partially masked example overall, reports repetitions/allocations and rejects infeasible coverage.
A+/B still start from the same A adapter and share training settings; all token categories and sampling
costs are reported separately.

**Proposed stage rule:** stop at 1,500 unless it beats 500 by at least **five percentage points** on the
same 96-task primary strict dev metric. That requires at least five net additional successes (5/96 =
5.208 points). Use unrounded scores; report fallback and shop retention too. This is a spending rule,
not significance evidence or paid approval. A tie means no difference detectable at this size.
